# LabLens – Custom RPi Enclosure

This folder contains the 3D printable enclosure designed to securely mount the Raspberry Pi 5 and Camera Module for stable lab deployment.

The design was inspired by creators **StealthNode** and **sl1zer**, and adapted for LabLens's specific mounting and camera angle requirements.

https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7354846
---

## Files

| File | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `Case.stl` | 3D printable enclosure model (RPi 5 body + camera mount + swivel bracket) |

---

## Assembly Requirements

### Top & Bottom RPi 5 Enclosure
- (4x) M2 x 16 Hex Socket Screws

### Swivel Bracket
- RPi 5 Enclosure Side: (2x) M3 x 8 Hex Socket Screws
- RPi Camera Enclosure Side: (2x) M3 x 12 Hex Socket Screws

### RPi Camera Enclosure
- (4x) M2 x 16 Hex Socket Screws
- (4x) M2 Nuts

---

## Printing Notes

- Print with standard PLA or PETG for durability
- Recommended layer height: 0.2mm
- Supports may be needed depending on orientation
- Ensure camera ribbon cable has clearance before fully assembling
